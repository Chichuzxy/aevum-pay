Enter a resource to load its price.

What happens on-chain

  1. approve — the token lets the AevumPay contract move up to your cap.
  2. createCard — the card records spender, merchant, cap and expiry.
  3. charge — the agent pays per unit; the cap and expiry are enforced by the contract.

Why the payer pays nothing

tUSD8 implements receiveWithAuthorization (EIP-3009). Your signature authorises one exact transfer; the contract only forwards it if the card still has room.
